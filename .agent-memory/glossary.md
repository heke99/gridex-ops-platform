# Glossary

- OPS: Gridex canonical operations platform.
- Tenant/company: electricity retailer isolated by `company_id`.
- Resolution: tenant-bound evidence for price/grid/facility context.
- Pricing ready: sufficient area/evidence for market price; independent of
  PRODAT route.
- Quote ready: sufficient resolution for canonical offer calculation.
- Switch creatable: durable switch request may be created.
- Switch dispatch ready: all transport/business prerequisites permit dispatch.
- Quote: immutable pre-contract calculation snapshot.
- Settlement: final locked calculation from actual/approved metering evidence.
- Billing underlay: locked invoicing basis; not an invoice.
- Outbox: durable post-commit external-effect intent.
