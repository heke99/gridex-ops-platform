# Product and users

Gridex OPS provides tenant onboarding, public product/quote/application APIs,
customer operations, legal evidence, facility data, supplier switching,
EDIEL/PRODAT/UTILTS, metering, settlement, billing underlays, invoice handoff,
customer portal projections and platform operations.

Primary users are tenant electricity-company staff, Gridex platform admins,
external tenant website backends and end customers through tenant-owned
surfaces. Tenant-facing language must be simple Swedish; diagnostics remain
available to authorized technical/platform roles.
