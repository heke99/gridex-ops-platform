The existing UTILTS comparison consumes the composed blocker and emits internal review, never a national mismatch from local uncertainty.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, PostgreSQL/Supabase forward migration and RPC, existing disposable native replay.

## Self-review and unresolved issues

The plan covers raw concern visibility, all three C-PB-1 owner sets, retention/failed-reader scope, earliest plausible boundary, cutoff immutability and real comparison hold. It deliberately does not implement C-PB-2 positive basis, its new permissioned semantic review, successful reopening, corrected new-Z05 qualification, retroactive exception, linked valid rescission resolution, multi-message, agency 89, delegation or pre-ledger reconstruction.

The concrete unresolved producer issue is historical outbound Z08H: current pre-send payload storage is best-effort and sent status/event is mutable; old sends cannot be certified by a new hook. A second issue is process-history retention initiation: current policy categories and mutable customer/contract rows do not prove complete historical coverage. The first slice must emit `complete:false` for those instances. A separate exact legal retention classification for newly captured correction documents may need owner decision before operational rollout; do not default to indefinite storage or a telemetry purge rule. The named files and interfaces are an execution proposal; producer discovery may reveal a narrower hook or a missing capture path that requires scoped plan amendment and review. None of these gaps blocks proving the hold-only behavior with genuine synthetic producers.
