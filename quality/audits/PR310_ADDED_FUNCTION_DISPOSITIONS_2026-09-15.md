## Concrete boundaries and remaining execution work

The seven relationship/assertion functions are explicitly restored by `20260905141608_canonical_tenant_relationship_guards.sql:1–8,12–229`. Six return trigger and only operate in trigger context; their reads remain invoker reads. Attachments require their own table/trigger privileges. Keep the prior audit’s explicit nullable/missing-parent limits and independent FK/RLS checks; an EXECUTE grant does not turn them into ordinary data-writing RPCs.

The number aliases deliberately require service_role and trust the authorized server tenant context. The two company inventory functions likewise expose platform-wide diagnostic rows only to service/owner. Their explicit scope makes preserving restricted definers appropriate; authenticated/public grants are not proposed. The readiness writer cannot manufacture success from only a caller-supplied fingerprint: its manifest/readiness checks remain required.

For invoker role and override projections, supplied user IDs are not a definer bypass. Actual table ACLs and RLS determine visible rows. Current authenticated dashboard use supplies its verified user; generic RPC argument flexibility does not itself prove cross-tenant disclosure. Conversely, this source review does not establish that every underlying RLS policy is correct or that recursive policy composition will execute successfully. Parent-owned actor qualification remains required.

No speculative fixes are proposed for the following source differences: the two writable-company helpers treat NULL status differently; the inbound Ediel resolver intentionally includes prior validity windows; the critical audit trigger takes actor IDs from row metadata; the count helper returns only mutually exclusive buckets. Their behavior is recorded explicitly rather than collapsed into equivalent helper names. The known effective-permission override gap remains outside these new-definition decisions and must still be repaired through its separately tracked shared-evaluator change.

Required actual native follow-through: verify the matched definitions/security modes and effective named-role EXECUTE; call the current-user role projections under independent users and confirm table/RLS isolation; exercise trigger dispatch and error rollback on authorized/unauthorized parent graphs; keep service-only numbering/event/readiness execution controls. The Ediel registry wrapper and source query retain historical evidence semantics; normative protocol version acceptance remains the separate canonical version engine. No new migration/ACL repair is justified merely by these34 additions.
