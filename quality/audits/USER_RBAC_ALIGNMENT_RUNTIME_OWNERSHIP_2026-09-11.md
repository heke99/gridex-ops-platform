Minimum handling: keep `admin_customer_latest_contract_counts(text,text)` owner-private, revoking PUBLIC, anon, authenticated, service_role and effective inherited paths; retain whole C as immutable characterization authority. - Sum duplicate known partitions, including the two valid `none` rows. Unknown statuses do not acquire a fabricated known bucket; they still belong in the explicit `all` total. Thus the sum of displayed known buckets can be smaller than `all`.
