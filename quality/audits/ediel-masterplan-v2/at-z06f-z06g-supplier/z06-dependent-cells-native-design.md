## Shared assertions and nonduplicate controls

**A: Accepted control.** Use genuine ingress, actual case and first approval. Read committed own structural receipts/batches/effect; one object receipt with actual register-count effect, applicable physical CONTRL/APERAK and outbox bound to original UNB/BGM/LI/object/agency/firstLIN. Verify actual F expectation for the qualified reading change and no G expectation. Replay must preserve immutable effects, receipts, original bytes, ACK identities and outbox. Review timestamps may advance. No E66 fulfillment matrix is duplicated.

**N: National missing/invalid own field.** Maintain valid EDIFACT envelope/counts; classify with actual complete canonical/source owners. No own structural first-effect receipt, customer/supply/contract/series write, reading expectation or positive application ACK. Verify permitted physical responses and exact own field/object correlation. Source validation, case/audit diagnostics and lawful negative ACK writes are distinct from forbidden business effects. Do not turn a missing native prerequisite, local hold or hard fixture assertion into a passing protocol-negative test.

**X: Received excluded information.** Preserve the extra bytes and immutable original/hash. Verify source-owned ignored occurrences where the actual owner emits the facet; require no business use of the excluded value. Otherwise coherent accepted structure may produce A. Syntax, source placement, party and register-chain gates continue to apply. Absence of a consumer effect alone is not persisted-ignore proof.

**U: Unknown.** Use genuinely unavailable ordinary input knowledge. Never forge source/permission/legal/readiness authority or infer false from a missing fact. Outgoing construction requiring unresolved facts stops. Receiving-local uncertainty is not automatically a sender national defect; observe actual classification and prevent effects that require the missing qualification. An inactive parent short-circuits an unknown child condition.

**U223: Shared subtype-unknown probe.** Missing/duplicate/noncanonical own first-register CCI Z13/CAV reason cannot borrow subtype from parsed cache, another object, later register or a sibling UNH. Preserve one original message and evaluate all affected cells in the same owner invocation. No separate fixture/resolver per cell.

Every physical omission/value contrast targets the exact own scope and keeps the envelope valid using the existing physical recount utility. Every active-parent omission needs an intact same-parent control. Snapshot own and actual foreign tenant/customer/site/point scope, original/public raw/hash/context, business rows, receipt/expectation/observation and ACK/outbox identity before any proposed first effect. Existing fixture baseline history is not zeroed.
