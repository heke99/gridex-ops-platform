## Initial supported process and exclusions

Support **network-owner correction of an erroneous move-in date**, single physicalZ04LK→Z04C→new originalZ04LK, same tenant/environment, point/customer/site, legal parties, original outboundZ03LK and LI. Distinct source/document IDs; exact market timestamps from original bytes. Initial positive slice uses agency9/direct sender, dates at market midnight(+0100), and both old/new starts still in the future when each operational transition is committed. Midnight and pre-effective limits are conservative support bounds; they are not claims that the cited clauses prohibit every other case.

Do not treat supplier-originated changed conditions/Z03C/newZ03, move-out-before-start with no replacement, internal-system correction, retroactive/already-active supply, changed customer/contract/point, invalid contract authority or changed-start BGM5 as this basis. Preserve them as scoped unavailable pending their specific owner. A reviewed basis is about why the old move-in date was wrong; three syntactically plausible messages alone do not establish that cause. No additional approval ceremony or market communication is part of this task.

Reuse the **architecture**, not closure-specific legal conclusions, of `cancellation-process-basis-design.md`: private sealed process artifacts, separately witnessed provenance assessment, typed basis assessment with narrowly permissioned authenticated reviewer and exact content locators. No inspected owner already produces an authenticated wrong-move-in-date finding. A new `reviewed-move-in-start-correction-basis-v1` must bind:

- original outboundZ03LK original/hash/LI and original acceptedZ04LK source/hash/physical object/assessment/facts/witness;
- actualZ04C original/hash/object and intended newZ04LK original/hash/object when received;

A cancellation can be observed before the explanatory basis or replacement arrives. ## CS-1 — mandatory process-history universe

A positive basis requires a complete **bounded retained process universe**, not only the reviewer-selected originalZ03 and three received messages. Introduce a dedicated `startCorrectionHistory` reader/capture contract; the closure owner's outboundZ08H reader cannot satisfy it. `lib/ediel/matching.ts::findMatchingSupplierSwitchRequest` may propose candidates, but its supplied-ID/newest-reference/limit1 result is not history completeness.

Mandatory independent history readers are:

1. The exact original outboundZ03LK, its sealed original/hash/LI, intent/request identity, linked switch/customer/site/point, legal sender/receiver, environment, actual dispatch attempts and acknowledged transport/process outcomes.
2. All retained outboundZ03C potentially targeting that request and all potentially superseding/newZ03 requests for the same point/customer/process interval, including original bytes, LI and other authentic correlation links, creation/preparation/cancellation states, dispatch attempts and sent/failed/uncertain outcomes. A failed draft does not prove a sent cancellation, but a missing/uncertain dispatch outcome must remain uncertain. Do not select only status=sent or only already-linked rows.
3. All retained inboundZ04 originals/C/new confirmations for the affected point/process, including canonical rejection/manual-review and unlinked or partially linked sources. Include physical geometry/source hashes, receipt/availability and current assessment predecessor chains, not mutable status alone.
4. Linked supplier-switch/request lifecycle and relevant contract/customer change/correction history, including evidence of who initiated changed conditions, actual signed/amended authority where relevant, conflicts and the qualified counterparty correction artifacts/provenance. Generic metadata contributes signals, not proof that it is unrelated.

Each reader returns a closed tenant/environment-scoped receipt naming its required universe, query scope, cutoff, exact observed count, inspected count, truncation/read-failure flag, source references/hashes and authenticated retention-coverage interval plus any known missing spans. Scope includes exact point/customer/legal parties and initiator-aware case relationships: Z03C targets its cancelled request LI; a newZ03 can have a different LI and must not be excluded solely because LI differs. Require coverage from the original request/process creation through the proposed basis/transition cutoff, with relevant prior linked context explicitly included. Database row count equality only proves that bounded query was read; it does not prove deletion-free retention. Use actual immutable outbound/process capture and its real coverage epoch/continuity records. If this infrastructure is missing, implement it prospectively; do not invent retrospective success or backfill unknown deleted history.

The current inbound-only source ledger does not contain outboundZ03C/new-request originals. Add explicit immutable captures/readers for this required outbound and operational history, or consume a demonstrated existing immutable producer only after its full record/dispatch/coverage semantics are verified. A generic current `ediel_messages` SELECT or document statement “network correction” is insufficient by itself. Retain rows with unresolved links/malformed potentially relevant identity as uncertainty at the narrowest proven scope. Unknown initiator/customer/point components cannot act as an exclusion filter. If exact point is not established, the receipt must report the unresolved broader scope; the branch cannot claim complete unrelatedness.

For a positive network-owner basis, qualified retained history must establish the selected request remains the operative request for this correction and no unresolved supplier-initiated cancellation/new-request conflict affects it. Positively identified supplierZ03C/newZ03 belongs to its separate owner and holds this branch. Missing required reader, unavailable provenance, truncation, retention gap, ambiguous relationship or unresolved dispatch status also holds even with a qualified explanatory document. This is bounded completeness of authentic retained evidence, not a claim of universal real-world completeness. All receipts and their cutoffs/coverage gaps enter the immutable basis facts and are rechecked for fresh transition eligibility; later contradictory/unwitnessed revisions prevent later fallback.

Proofs: genuine positive artifact plus actual conflicting dispatchedZ03C/newZ03 cannot qualify; same positive artifact plus failed/truncated/missing outbound-history reader or missing retention span cannot qualify. A different-point/different-process history positively resolved as unrelated must not poison this point. Use genuinely captured synthetic originals/dispatch events and real reader failure injection; no mocked successful completeness receipt standing in for the native history owner.

## CS-2 — durable pending-withdrawal guard before qualification

Add a private durable `pending_start_withdrawal_concerns` registry, separate from positive basis and transition tables. A concern is an operational activation block derived from an observed sealed source; it is **not** a lawful-cancellation decision, accepted owner or new positive lifecycle phase. It binds tenant/environment, exact originalC hash/physical scope, observed identity/parties/LI, candidate point and all potentially affected switch/period IDs or explicit unresolved linkage, plus DB capture time and reason. Capture rejected/unreviewed/unwitnessed C concerns conservatively when their physical context may affect an existing start. No supply date/status or positive source acceptance is changed by this guard.

Establish concern capture as part of the supported source intake/correlation transaction, before an originalC can become an unguarded review item. Extend the actual database intake/capture boundary for PRODAT with a bounded source-derived cancellation concern projection; do not rely on a later JS callback, actor-testing hook or eventual readset classification. The existing original sealing is reused and the concern references those bytes. Capture exact point candidate set from original identity+tenant/environment+party evidence; when switch/LI linkage is ambiguous, block all potentially affected starts at that **point**, never choose latest matching switch. A provably unrelated point receives no guard. If physical/party identity cannot safely bound a potentially relevant concern to a point, record unresolved intake scope and prevent claims of complete safe activation readiness for that scope until it is resolved; do not guess a point or silently drop it. This unresolved condition is not approval or a national rejection.

Define one shared locking protocol for intake guard, direct activation, scheduled activation and both qualified correction operations: acquire tenant/environment/point activation mutex, then affected switch/period locks in deterministic order. For unresolved broader intake scope, use a documented enclosing readiness barrier whose lock order precedes point locks, with no broad-then-narrow inversion. The source-capture transaction must acquire the relevant mutex before publishing the concern/source as committed. Activation acquires the same mutex then performs a **fresh database statement** checking current immutable source/concern state, unresolved-scope barrier, explicit withdrawal state and switch eligibility before any supply activation. It may not reuse a snapshot read before waiting for that lock. Both `activate_customer_supply_v1` and `gridex_finalize_supplier_switch_activation`, and the scheduler's actual invoked mutation path, independently enforce this guard; filtering the scheduler query alone is insufficient.

If intake/guard capture fails, the supported intake operation must roll back or durably establish an explicit unresolved readiness barrier in the same committed boundary; it cannot publish a source plus successful safe-readiness claim with no concern. No unsupported asynchronous eventual catch-up window. If activation wins the serialized race before the concern is captured, record the concern and classify subsequent correction as outside the pre-start slice; do not backdate withdrawal or erase the activation. If concern capture wins, activation cannot proceed until a qualified resolution covers it.

The guard has no TTL/automatic removal and persists for missing/rejected/unwitnessed/incomplete basis/history states. It is resolved by an append-only, authorized, original-bound resolution that identifies **that particular source/physical concern**: either qualified Operation1 takes over with durable withdrawn/lifecycle-block state, or a separately evidence-backed nonapplicability resolution proves the concern irrelevant to the named start. Canonical rejection alone does not establish that a stated cancellation concern is irrelevant. Clearing one concern cannot clear another sibling/source concern. Operation2 must ensure all relevant concerns are resolved or covered by its qualified lineage before unblocking activation. UI review may expose this state but cannot toggle it away.

Operational guard effectiveness starts at its committed intake boundary and does not wait for the later comparison availability witness. Structural comparison separately retains the raw scopedC blocker and its own historical source cutoff; this guard is not new positive inventory. The same original is used to bind both, with no conflation of operational safety and witnessed business authority.

Native/actual-flow proofs: source intake of potentially applicableC with missing basis/history→advance scheduler to old start and invoke **both** direct activation functions→no activation, no changed supply dates and no false withdrawal owner; actual concurrent C capture versus activation in both lock order outcomes; exact unrelated-point control still activates normally; ambiguous switch at the same point blocks all candidates; source/guard write failure leaves no unguarded complete readiness; one concern's qualified resolution leaves another pending. Exercise public intake and real database mutation functions, not only guard predicates. Retain the qualified-operation race tests already below.

## Two atomic operations, one immutable correction lineage

Use a private append-only `start_correction_transitions` owner with explicit phases `start_withdrawn` and `corrected_start_confirmed`, each referencing the previous phase, old root, originals, basis and before/after operational snapshots. IDs and facts hashes are DB-owned; direct DML forbidden. A distinct source/object/case idempotency key and row locks prevent double cancellation/reconfirmation or forks. Do not rely on source LI alone, because LI is deliberately reused here.

**Operation1 — withdraw original start:** authenticate caller/current tenant rights; require CS-1 history receipts and acquire the CS-2 shared activation locks; lock source originals, basis, switch, exact original supply and point/site; independently prove full canonical originalZ04LK/C, target identity/parties/LI and witnessed post-ledger root. Require switch still accepted, period still confirmed_by_grid_owner, both dates exactly old source210 and no actual activation/billing consequence. Reject multiple plausible periods, conflicting closure/correction or changed owner graph. Atomically set switch to its existing cancelled-before-start state plus explicit transition pointer/lifecycle block; set that exact period to a dedicated non-delivering `cancelled_start` state, retaining its original start and source fields for provenance; append withdrawal transition with prior graph and source facts. Do not invent an end_date: withdrawal is not aZ05 end. Qualify all consumers of the new supply state before activation. Existing text status column does not by itself make consumers understand cancellation.

**Operation2 — confirm corrected start:** require witnessed qualified withdrawal, complete basis and fresh CS-1 history coverage; independently bind new canonical originalZ04LK to same originalZ03LK LI and graph, different source/document and changed exact210. Validate new date against retained actual contract authority; if the contract requires an amendment that lacks its authentic owner, hold. Re-lock exact switch/period, check unchanged withdrawal state and new start still future, and conflict-check all other periods/switches for the point. Atomically update the *same* cancelled period UUID to new start_date, status confirmed_by_grid_owner, source=newZ04, correct source_switch_request_id and contract linkage; `actual_start_date` stays null in this pre-start slice. Update switch confirmed_start_date/new inboundZ04 and appropriate lifecycle readiness, preserving original requested_start_date and outboundZ03/LI. Append immutable second transition with old/new snapshots and fresh actual commit evidence. No duplicate supply period; do not invoke covering-period `limit1` reuse or overwrite immutable original assessment.

Reuse the existing supply/contract constraints and actual business authorization. Include notification/workflow side effects only through existing durable transactional/outbox contracts; failure cannot leave switch and supply disagreeing. Both operations return a genuine commit capability/transition ID to the source ledger; append and separate availability witness qualify knowledge. DB commit without witness does not create comparison authority. Retry reconstructs from an actual immutable transition, not mutable status. New replacement arriving before a witnessed withdrawal stays pending; no inferred ordering from document IDs or caller clocks.

The activation functions/job must lock and honor this transition state before any activation. Cancellation holds prevent scheduler activation of the old date; corrected confirmation activates only from new date under retained rules. A transaction racing activation serializes on switch/period; if activation wins, the pre-start correction rejects/holds. Freeze already-written billing/quantity consequences: initial slice requires none, and does not delete or retrospectively rewrite them.

## Coverage, knowledge and actual UTILTS consumer

Represent start withdrawal separately from closure. At cutoffs before withdrawal availability, saved reads see the original witnessed structure subject to other then-known blockers. At cutoffs after withdrawal, the old baseline contributes **no positive delivery interval**, including intervals from its old start: cancellation says it did not start, not that it ended on the cancellation receipt date. No future witness rewrites a saved earlier snapshot or a finalized successful quantity/response.

Until new confirmation and its owner are witnessed, the affected root has a scoped coverage hold. When second transition is witnessed, new baseline starts at the new original210, with its own meter/register inventory and exact graph lineage. Moving later leaves `[oldStart,newStart)` without positive coverage; never fall back to original there. Moving earlier is eligible only from newStart if it is supported by the new genuine source, after ledger start and within proven outbound/switch/contract timing. A negative/unwitnessed latest transition cannot fall back to old acceptance. Unknown physical/party scope stays conservatively unresolved.
