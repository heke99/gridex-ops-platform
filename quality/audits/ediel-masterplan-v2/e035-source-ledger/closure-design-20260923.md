# Minimal coherent E035 closure design

Read-only design against 95b5e391. No implementation or acceptance claim. Frozen P hash independently verified; P13/50/65/123 read directly. Source evidence permits Z05L/Z22 and Z05LK/Z23 inbound stop, exact object DTM93/203, optional prior Z08H, and cancellation LI matching the cancelled original. Existing final-reading side rules are retained; no new U interpretation is proposed.

## Decision

Use a **new reviewed closure business marker in the existing immutable object assessment and separate availability witness**, distinct from `reviewed-received-structure-v1`. A Z05 closes coverage; it never supplies meter/register inventory. This can be the next bounded implementation without redesigning the legacy lifecycle or creating an invented historic owner.

Minimal accepted scope: single physical PRODAT, agency9, direct legal sender, no subaddresses, L/LK, original function9/null, unchanged start, existing witnessed reviewed post-ledger Z04 baseline and genuine committed root. Require the live legacy supply to already show this exact closure source and ended status. BGM5, Z05C, bilateral Z05H, changed-start, pre-ledger and missing anchors remain explicit scoped holds. This is not full closure/cancellation completion; the marker format must leave those distinctions visible.

## 1. Preserve a genuine anchor

The existing committed root (`receivedSourceOwnerSession.ts`) stores switch ID, supply ID, customer/point/site, original source/hash, exact wire210 and committed start dates. SQL `object_owner_proof_consistent` and append-time live join checked the actual switch/supply at commit, followed by the separate witness. That immutable root survives `endActiveSupplyPeriod` overwriting the live supply source_message_id/status.

However, this root alone does **not** store the complete post-ledger coverage proof (outbound Z03 ID/times, epoch relationship). The minimum closure owner therefore requires an already witnessed `reviewed-received-structure-v1` Z04 assessment containing `coverageWindow`. Its baseline root ID/hash must resolve to the genuine committed owner above. Select it from a saved complete review snapshot; require the latest applicable assessment to remain accepted and witnessed by the snapshot cutoff. Never fall back past a later unwitnessed/rejected/unavailable revision.

Persist BOTH the immutable committed root reference and the witnessed reviewed coverage assessment reference (ID/factsHash/sourcePayloadHash). Require exact identity matches across marker, root, reviewed baseline and closure: tenant, environment, physical object+agency, legal FR/DO, customer, point, site, switch, supply. The reviewed baseline must refer to its own Z04 source and its own committed root. No newest UUID/receipt selection.

At closure review, re-read current same-ID switch, supply and outbound rows. Validate unchanged switch accepted/inbound_z04/source case/start/customer/point/site, unchanged supply start/customer/point/source_switch_request_id, and retained outbound Z03 identity/dates/ownership against immutable coverage. Accept only expected supply mutation: status ended, source_message_id equals *this* closure source, end_date equals market-calendar date projection of closure DTM93. If live source points to a later Z05C or another closure, hold. Match exactly one baseline/supply; do not reuse the legacy latest-open-row heuristic as proof.

Missing previously reviewed baseline is not automatically external evidence: a future explicit requalification owner could establish its missing coverage from authentic retained root+rows. It is outside this minimal slice. Do not issue a new ordinary Z04 review by weakening its current-source/status checks, and do not rewrite old immutable assessments.

## 2. Exact date authority

New `readClosureSourceWire(raw, scope)` returns original document/case references, function, L/LK/C/other reason, full legal/transport tuple, and `effectiveTo:{fieldNumber:'211',marketMinute,utc}`. Use actual per-object first/common DTM93 exactly once, format203, strict calendar/time validation, DTMZZZ=1 and existing fixed-standard-time converter. Validate complete physical membership with the canonical scope; do not derive from mutable payload dates. No DTM92/157/start_date fallback. Never fabricate meter/register fields to reuse `StructuralSourceWire`.

Exact stop authority is the freshly reviewed original + SQL-validated immutable closure marker. The legacy SQL DATE only corroborates the calendar projection and successful business attribution; explicitly call it `legacyEndDateProjection`, not an exact business instant. The new owner approves exact structural coverage closure and is committed by append_object_assessment. It does not claim that every downstream billing/lifecycle consumer has gained sub-day precision. Add that limitation to the marker's closed semantics and documentation; expanding legacy billing precision is a separate task.

SQL first derives the closure wire independently from the sealed original using the mandatory mechanism in section 3A below. Comparing a caller-provided minute only to its own UTC value or to the live DATE is insufficient. After original-wire equality, SQL reconstructs `make_timestamp(...) AT TIME ZONE 'Etc/GMT-1'` from the 12 digits and compares exact timestamptz, rejects infinity/invalid calendar/time, and compares legacy DATE to the market timestamp DATE, never UTC DATE. Require stop > baseline start. Prior coverage validTo must be null or identical stop; do not silently extend/shrink a pre-existing unexplained end.

## 3. Closed marker and SQL contract

Suggested owner `reviewed-received-closure-v1`, `coverage:'reviewed_post_ledger_closure'`, businessDisposition reviewed, sourceDisposition not_established; same common source/object/reviewer/assessedAt/graph IDs/party fields as reviewed structure. Additional closed fields:

- wire (closure type only, exact effectiveTo and original case identity)
- reviewStatement `original_supply_closure`
- baselineCoverageAssessment {sourceMessageId,assessmentId,factsHash,payloadHash}
- coverageWindow (original immutable baseline window, not edited)
- baselineCurrentAssessmentId
- reviewSnapshot {snapshotId,readsetHash,cutoffAt}
- legacyEndDateProjection (DATE string), no copied `approved:true`

Add `isReviewedClosureBusiness` that reparses original wire and compares complete closed shape. Use an explicit review action/server path and fresh canonical runtime receipt, same reviewer permissions and tenant environment filtering as current whole-original structural review. This is authorization for the *original closure*, separate from partial safe-apply and from approval of all Z05 messages. Do not send market messages or write operational dates from the review action.

CLI-created forward migration adds `review_closure_proof_consistent`; it independently checks reviewer permission/company status, snapshot source/hash membership, witnessed root and coverage assessments, latest root/review acceptance, complete identities, exact effectiveTo, live switch/supply/outbound graph and timestamp predicates in one STABLE/MVCC owner-validation SELECT. Reuse `review_party_proof_consistent` and all `owner_rows_match` checks unchanged. `owner_rows_match` currently covers profile/identifier/role/relation/transportIdentifier/point/site/gridOwner rows via bidirectional EXCEPT ALL; it does not verify supply/switch/outbound. Therefore new helper must explicitly verify those three live rows, not assume the generic checker does so.

Forward-replace append_object_assessment only to add an explicit third owner branch in: (a) allowed businessDisposition, (b) proof-consistent CASE, (c) the Z04-specific live-join bypass. The bypass must name both reviewed owners explicitly, not allow arbitrary non-Z04 owners. Keep canonical accepted/register accepted/all physical objects, source-hash bindings, exact party count, owner_readsets, source serialization, predecessor and witness contracts unchanged. No new broad grants; private helper denied direct public/anon/authenticated/service invocation as current pattern. Existing public RPC signature and JSONB storage suffice, so public generated types should remain byte-identical unless an intentional new RPC is chosen; schema snapshot changes honestly.

## 3A. Mandatory append-time original-wire binding (review correction)

**The initial design omitted a necessary trust boundary.** Current canonical facts persist physical segment indices but not source-owned DTM93/LI values. Existing SQL explicitly does not parse EDIFACT, and searches found no reusable release-aware SQL EDIFACT parser. Therefore a marker minute or case reference plus caller-supplied payload hash is not enough: a forged 12:35 instead of original 12:34 shares the legacy DATE and otherwise passes. Do not implement the preceding helper without this independent binding.

Concrete minimum: add private bounded SQL helpers in the same forward migration:

1. `gridex_received_sources.closure_wire_tokens_v1(p_raw text) RETURNS jsonb` (or an equivalent private typed token array). Pure IMMUTABLE, fixed search_path, no table access. It reads the *actual original text*, understands default UNA and a complete explicit nine-character UNA, and lexes segment/data/component separators with a release-character state machine. Produce an array of physical segments, each containing a zero-based index, tag and nested arrays of decoded elements/components. Retain physical boundaries; never split strings after release decoding. This is a narrow evidence parser, not a replacement canonical validator.
2. `gridex_received_sources.closure_wire_matches_v1(p_raw text,p_object jsonb,p_wire jsonb) RETURNS boolean`. Independently reconstruct the supported closure projection from these tokens, then compare all authority-relevant fields and complete object geometry to p_object/p_wire. A helper returning the reconstructed wire JSON is also acceptable, with exact equality performed by the caller. Any unsupported/malformed/over-budget form returns false, never a guessed projection.
3. `review_closure_proof_consistent` obtains `src.raw_payload` itself from `gridex_received_sources.sources` by the already validated source ID/company/environment/hash. It invokes this helper with that sealed column, never a raw string supplied in the marker/RPC. Do not allow a caller to choose another original, an alleged DTM segment, token list or offset as authority.

Lexer behavior must be specified and tested, not approximated with regexp/split_part. Read UNA before any normalization. Default separators are the existing TS defaults; explicit UNA uses its actual component/data/decimal/release/reserved-or-repetition/terminator characters. Require complete UNA and non-colliding active component/data/release/terminator separators; unsupported advice remains held. Match the canonical tokenizer's CRLF/LF normalization and segment trimming only where needed for token-index parity, and explicitly reject problematic forms rather than normalize extra bytes silently. Scan code points once; a release consumes the next character as literal data, even if it is a terminator/data/component separator or release character. A dangling release fails. Preserve empty/trailing components and elements. Treat syntax-version repetition conservatively: minimal closure projection supports no repetitions in authoritative fields; reject advice/forms requiring a repetition grammar the helper does not implement. A released UNA/UNH-like sequence inside data must never open a new message. Bound original bytes (suggest 262144 for this slice), segments (4096), decoded component length (4096) and LIN objects (16), with overflow false before excessive allocation. These are support limits, not invented national rejection codes.

Projection must derive, rather than trust indices:

- Exactly one actual UNB/UNH/UNT/UNZ physical message; UNH family PRODAT and original reference, BGM unique in header, code Z05, document reference and actual function9/null. No BGM after LIN and no nested message. Parse UNT/UNZ structural consistency or require it in addition to genuine canonical accepted; never let a fake in-field UNH affect count.
- Header before first LIN contains exactly one FR and one DO, actual qualifier160/agencySVK. UNB sender/receiver identifiers and qualifiers come from their original composites; retain the existing direct-sender/no-subaddress supported restrictions. Compare all derived party values to marker wire and party owner.
- Enumerate every actual LIN using the same zero-based global segment indices and lineIndex as prodatRegisterGroups. Each supported closure object has exactly one LIN occurrence (Z05 is not register-repeating Z04/Z06/Z10). Derive original identifier and agency from LIN C212, line number from LIN314, reject register sequence C829, and require messageIndex0/messageReference plus complete registers geometry to equal canonical object. Never select the LIN solely by supplied segmentIndex or supplied object ID.
- Scope selected object's body from its own LIN to next LIN/UNT. Its common prefix before first RFF/NAD must contain exactly one DTM93 with precisely original minute/format203 and one CCI++Z13 immediately followed by its own CAV carrying Z22 or Z23. Its reference region before first NAD must contain exactly one RFF+LI with original decoded case reference. No cross-object or header inheritance. Canonical validation continues to own all other required/forbidden fields; local marker source-binding does not waive it.
- Derive DTMZZZ=1 from the actual message header. Compare exact original twelve-digit DTM93 to `effectiveTo.marketMinute`, derive UTC independently, compare fieldNumber211; compare original reason/subtype, documentReference, caseReference, function and all source party/object fields. Any marker key not allowed by the closed type fails. Keep marker-only review/coverage metadata out of the derived wire.

This consciously duplicates only the small lexical/source-binding surface at the database trust boundary. TS remains the semantic/canonical owner, SQL prevents a forged composition from asserting values absent in its immutable original. A stored caller-provided digest/token projection, even inserted via the same service role, would merely move the same trust gap and is not an alternative solution. A future database-owned parser-produced projection could replace direct parsing, but only if generated from the sealed original by the database itself.

Mandatory native mutations (all against a real accepted original and real otherwise-valid owner rows): change 12:34 to12:35 **and update utc accordingly**, retain same DATE; change only RFF+LI; change documentReference, reason Z22/Z23, function9/5, object/agency, legal party, transport tuple or scope segmentIndex; borrow valid DTM/LI from another LIN; supply released delimiter text that looks like another DTM/UNH. Each append must fail, no witness/assessment creation. Positive native tests include custom valid UNA, escaped delimiter in LI/document reference, escaped release, released terminator, CRLF-formatted wire and multiple distinct closure objects, plus boundary/overflow/dangling-release negatives. Compare SQL-derived wire to production TS projection on those fixtures, but retain hand-written exact expected values so both cannot agree on the same bug. Add red native assertions that forged same-date minute/case must be rejected: observe those assertions fail with the binding absent, then pass with the binding installed. Retain the mutation probes in ordinary replay.

## 4. Readset and timeline projection

Add `closures:ClosureVersion[]` and `closureBlockers:ScopedClosureBlocker[]`; keep global unresolvedSources only for evidence whose scope truly cannot be bounded. `ClosureVersion` includes source/hash/assessment/factsHash/availability/disposition, exact wire, anchor coverage reference, and validated marker. Extend receivedSourceDecisionTimeline.observedObjects accepted-owner branch to recognize the new marker; preserve physical bind, canonical membership and time constraints. Accepted marker alone never skips this existing timeline check.

The source ledger already contains Z05 and physical LIN evidence even though named register scopes; verify an actual canonical electricity Z05 fixture produces accepted physical projection. Do not waive register facet acceptance if it does not: locate/fix the actual projection owner with source-backed tests first.

In inspectStructuralReadset, classify Z05 *before* its current unsupported-code global flag. Parse scope independently of approval so an unavailable/rejected physical closure can become a scoped blocker. For every physical object preserve an entry. Valid exact object+agency+FR/DO and exact DTM93 permits a blocker limited to that object and lower bound. Unknown date with known object limits to that object at all times. Unknown party cannot justify excluding the same object; treat missing components as wildcards, not mismatches. Unknown object/physical membership still requires global hold. Malformed raw source never disappears because a guessed parser result says unrelated.

Z05C must not enter a no-effect skip list. In this slice it is an unsupported cancellation blocker, scoped to its physical object/parties and original end boundary. Its same LI may identify an already read closure, but do not treat C as reopening without its own reviewed owner. BGM5 may move stop later/earlier: without an accepted explicit predecessor, use the earliest potentially affected matching boundary (or whole object interval when unbounded), never just its proposed date. Bilateral Z05H/other reasons remain visible as unsupported scoped blockers.

## 5. Selection and impacted time

Thread closures/blockers through `qualifyReceivedStructure` -> `compareUtiltsStructure` -> `selectStructuralSources`. First establish existing candidate supply coverage/inventory, then apply only closure markers whose full logical identity and immutable baseline coverage/root match that supply. Do not add closures to inventory versions or replacement graph.

A single fully witnessed accepted closure bounds effective coverage at exact stop. Before stop, preserve existing inventory selection. Existing closing_point at stop can use the pre-close structure; current_point at stop and later periods are outside coverage. Interval ending at stop is allowed, interval crossing stop unavailable. Return the effective bounded coverage plus closure provenance in diagnostics, without mutating stored baseline.

For valid scoped but unreviewed/rejected closure: hold only queries whose end reaches/passes its stop or whose point reaches/passes it; earlier queries and unrelated object/agency/legal-party queries remain unaffected. Conservatively include exact stop for blockers since the transition is not qualified. This is deliberate stronger fail-closed treatment than rejecting a structural inventory replacement: a rejected closure must not authorize continuing supply past an unresolved lifecycle boundary. Rejection never shortens or reopens supply as an accepted event.

Conflicting accepted closures, multiple distinct predecessors, cancelled/unsupported closure, or same-source later unwitnessed revision produce a scoped hold, not earliest/latest wins. Unknown lower bound affects the matching object throughout the requested interval. Keep immutable knowledge cutoff semantics: later received or later witnessed approvals cannot alter an older saved selection. Earlier known unavailable closure stays a hold at that earlier cutoff even if it is accepted later.

Run retained native suites and final ordinary CI; independent four-part review remains mandatory.

## Boundaries and actual blockers

Everything above is engineering with available source/owners. Prior reviewed coverage missing in a particular record is missing qualification, not permission; it remains held in this minimal slice. Authentic missing historic data, unrecorded real bilateral agreement, or actual delegation evidence cannot be manufactured. Final-reading business interpretation beyond current qualified U rules needs the correct frozen U/HB source, not a guessed new rule. No masterplan/closure-all-cases completion follows from this slice.
