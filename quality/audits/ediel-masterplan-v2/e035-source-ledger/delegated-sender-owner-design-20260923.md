**Goal:** admit a PRODAT structural source whose UNB sender is a genuinely authorized legal agent of its NAD sender, without treating actor configuration, a reviewer assertion or SMTP authentication as representation.

**Architecture:** add an immutable authority-evidence ledger and a source-specific sender owner. Both TypeScript and the append RPC independently resolve the same dated, scoped evidence. Persist the selected authority versions in the existing source assessment; downstream selection uses its immutable snapshot.

Skill routing: writing-plans for component sequencing; prior source/compliance and false-positive review workflow for evidence. This is a narrow design, not a repository-wide audit. Implementation remains sequential with the designated sole coder; no delegation or production edits are authorized by this document.

## 1. Capture genuine evidence before enabling acceptance

Add a separate capture boundary, proposed `lib/ediel/sources/senderAuthorityCapture.ts`, and private append-only SQL tables. Do not extend the general XML-upload API with a caller-controlled `approved` flag.

A qualifying capture must preserve exact original bytes, SHA256, issuer identity, acquisition channel and server observation time, provider record/version reference, parser/profile version and exact fragment/record locator. The evidence itself must establish principal, agent, legal representation (not merely SMTP routing), message-family/market/direction scope, and validity or historical status. Actor identity/EDI eligibility evidence must be independently linked for both parties. Record tenant/environment visibility explicitly; an authoritative public relationship does not make every company's source records visible.

Accepted acquisition class for this slice: a server-controlled fetch/capture from the qualified official directory, with authenticated origin and a documented record contract which actually identifies legal delegation and validity. The connector chooses the allowlisted origin and record key; callers cannot supply arbitrary URL, bytes, issuer, observedAt or validity. Retain response bytes and provenance before projecting records. Fetch/parser failures produce unavailable evidence. Credentials remain outside the evidence record. The capture writer has a dedicated database identity; ordinary service-role assessment callers cannot directly insert authority records or invoke its writer function. An RPC taking a JSON claim from the ordinary service role is not an authenticity boundary.

If the provider cannot supply historical dates, store an observation, not a backdated interval. It cannot qualify a previously received source by assuming the relationship existed then. A signed mandate is a possible separately qualified evidence adapter, but it needs issuer/signatory authority, scope and revocation verification; an uploaded PDF plus a reviewer click is not that adapter. Do not silently add it to this slice.

**Source qualification gap:** the supplied original guides describe the portal relationship but provide no real principal→agent record, authenticated acquisition contract, historical-validity fields, withdrawal semantics or actor-agreement status record. The existing upload parser does not establish those. Before enabling the positive path, obtain a redacted authentic representative capture and its official schema/field semantics and qualify the connector against it. Do not invent a portal endpoint, signature format, timestamps or historical backfill. A synthetic fixture can verify implementation mechanics but cannot close this evidence gap. This is a precise outstanding input, not a claim that evidence is externally unavailable.

## 2. Immutable records, dates and cardinality

Proposed private records:

- `sender_authority_captures`: immutable bytes/hash, capture provenance, issuer/profile, server-recorded capture/commit time, visibility, explicit validation result. Content hash remains a hash; reprocessing appends a new assessment, never a timestamp suffix to the hash.
- `sender_authority_versions`: capture FK plus record locator/hash, stable principal and agent actor bindings, exact Ediel identifier/qualifier evidence, market `electricity`, environment, direction `sending`, family `PRODAT`, allowed application scope, asserted `[valid_from,valid_to)`, knowledge/recorded time, supersedes/withdrawal link. Missing dates remain unknown. Explicitly separate source-stated time from system observation.

Use an independently qualified binding between selected `grid_owners.id` and the principal actor/Ediel identity; neither a matching name nor two unauthenticated uploaded ID strings suffices. Include this binding in the SQL readset. No actor ID merging or wider registry cleanup is part of this slice.

Policy: establish sender authority **at source receipt time**, using immutable `source_received_at`, not UNB local time, source effective supply date, review time or caller time. This is the conservative owner policy, not a claim that T mandates receipt rather than dispatch time. If a delegation changed between dispatch and receipt and the evidence cannot establish the selected instant, leave unavailable. Future dispatch-time support needs trustworthy dispatch-time evidence. Structural effectiveFrom/effectiveTo continues to govern supply/meter selection separately.

Knowledge cutoff is assessment creation time. Later acquired genuinely historical evidence may produce a new assessment of the same sealed original, with knowledge time recorded now; it cannot alter what a previous snapshot knew. Active-now relation alone does not prove authority at past receipt. Never extrapolate capture time backward. Expiry after receipt does not erase a formerly qualified source; a later withdrawal/correction is published with the mandatory snapshot barrier specified in §3a; future consumers cannot reuse the dependent accepted assessment while it is unresolved.

Select exactly one compatible authorization after applying explicit supersession as of cutoff. Two conflicting/overlapping candidates, even same agent with incompatible dates/scope, are unavailable; never `limit(1)` or latest-row guessing. UTILTS is outside this enabling slice; do not model per-TIM/SCH agent exceptions that would violate T5.2.2. Application reference narrows the proven scope; it never grants a relationship on its own.

## 3. Source-specific owner and SQL independence

Add `senderDelegationOwner.ts` with an internal resolver taking sealed source identity, complete physical object key, parsed original parties/application scope, selected facility, source receipt instant and assessment cutoff. Return either an explicit direct binding or `delegated-counterparty-sender-v1` with exact immutable capture/version IDs and hashes, temporal evaluation, complete candidate readset, actor binding evidence and reasons. Never accept a caller-supplied accepted owner.

Binding includes company/environment, source message UUID and payload hash, canonical assessment, physical message index/reference, object index/object identity agency, register scope, NAD FR/DO and full UNB sender/receiver composite components. Preserve existing one-message/agency9 limits. Subaddresses remain unavailable until their existing independent qualification gap is closed; do not truncate them to make a sender match. Direct sender retains existing facility checks and receives an explicit direct branch; receiver evidence stays unchanged.

Integrate resolver in all three production paths: committed `receivedSourceOwnerSession.ts`, `reviewReceivedStructuralSource.ts`, `reviewReceivedClosureSource.ts`. Keep commit/reviewer permission and coverage evidence requirements intact. Extract wire identity without imposing direct equality, then demand the correct owner branch. The three wire readers and the SQL original-wire parser must change together; rejecting unsupported components remains parser responsibility, legal delegation becomes owner responsibility.

Forward SQL migration must:

1. Create private immutable records with RLS/FORCE RLS, revoked direct writes, no mutation/truncate, strict hashes/FKs/scopes and restricted capture ingress.
2. Recompute original wire/source/hash/physical scope and NAD→facility owner binding; resolve authority versions itself from private tables under a fixed transaction snapshot. Compare complete candidate set and selected IDs/hashes with supplied evidence; do not accept JSON `is_verified`, `authority`, timestamps or selection as proof.
3. Apply receipt-time validity and knowledge-cutoff rules, reject gaps/ambiguity/scope mismatch and verify issuer/capture qualification. Record durable selected authority references in source assessment facts. Ensure concurrent capture/revocation is ordered by committed ledger visibility; no precommit timestamp can make later data appear in an earlier snapshot.
4. Preserve receiving tenant independent reads, roles and dated transport delegation. Extend `object_owner_proof_consistent` and `review_party_proof_consistent` through one internal sender proof routine while retaining their separate business checks. Remove direct equality only where that routine replaces it.
5. Preserve append-only assessment chaining/witnesses and whole-object coverage. A missing or invalid sender owner is `unavailable`, not a fabricated national negative ACK.

Old direct accepted assessments remain readable under their old version. Existing delegated originals gain no authority automatically: explicitly revalidate sealed bytes and append a new assessment only when genuine evidence qualifies. No historical delegated-row backfill. Snapshot consumers use persisted owner references; they must not look up the current directory again and rewrite old selection.

## 3a. Retrospective correction owner and atomic snapshot visibility (Q1)

Choose a **durable snapshot barrier**, not an asynchronous promise to rewrite all source assessments. Proposed private SQL owner `publish_sender_authority_revision` is the only publication path for a qualified replacement/withdrawal; `open_object_selection_snapshot` is the sole consumer that materializes its impact. The existing source timeline alone cannot discover an authority-ledger change.

Add immutable `sender_authority_dependencies` alongside each accepted delegated object, inserted by the same append transaction as its assessment. SQL derives each dependency from the independently selected authority, never a caller list: company/environment, source UUID/hash, assessment ID/facts hash, complete physical object key, selected authority version/capture and principal, source receipt instant. Require one complete dependency per accepted delegated object through the append function and integrity validation; no accepted delegated row may commit without it. Historical direct rows need no fabricated dependency.

Publish the new capture/version **and** an immutable `sender_authority_revision_events` row in one restricted database transaction. The event identifies the superseded version/principal, qualified correction evidence, asserted affected receipt interval or explicit unknown extent, and publication transaction identity. No intermediate state exposes a usable correction without its barrier. Unknown extent conservatively affects every dependent source of the superseded version/principal in its permitted visibility scope. A normal expiry starting after a source's receipt does not block that source when the earlier interval remains established. Missing/uncertain semantics cannot be labeled harmless expiry.

Extend the ONE MVCC statement inside `open_object_selection_snapshot` (`20260922150922_ediel_source_decision_snapshots.sql:50–91`) to read assessments, dependencies and committed revision events together. For each candidate source/object, join its selected assessment dependencies to visible events. Emit a versioned `senderAuthorityBlockers` array inside the immutable readset, including source/object/assessment identifiers, event/version hashes and reason; include dependency/event counts and a completeness marker in the hashed snapshot. An affected latest accepted object becomes unavailable **for this snapshot**, without altering the old assessment. Apply this overlay in `receivedSourceDecisionTimeline.ts`/`structuralSourceReadset.ts` before projecting accepted structural versions and closures; block the source identity against fallback to an older accepted assessment or predecessor. A compromised closure cannot become a harmless omitted closure that revives supply: retain an explicit affected-window blocker consumed by structural comparison.

Dependency discovery is a database join over all candidate accepted delegated objects for the requested company/environment, with exact counts; no background pagination or caller-selected impacted IDs. If a dependency is missing, a correction's principal/scope cannot be resolved, or event/dependency bounds overflow, mark the snapshot incomplete and return unavailable through the existing readset path. Do not emit a partial apparently complete list. Shared principal corrections are stored once with explicit visibility rules and independently joined in each authorized tenant/environment snapshot; publication must not rely on enumerating a currently known list of tenants. Cross-tenant payloads are never returned. The event foreign-key/reference model must allow the conservative principal barrier even where version-specific dependency matching is uncertain.

Publication and capture are atomic. A snapshot statement whose MVCC view precedes the correction commit may retain V1; one whose view follows the commit must include V2's barrier and cannot select affected V1. An assessment concurrently committing against V1 is still caught by its mandatory dependency in every subsequent snapshot; revalidating only during assessment append is insufficient. Record the committed event transaction and the consuming snapshot’s observation as the barrier visibility witness; no separate delayed witness is required before blocking. Positive replacement authority still requires its qualified availability evidence. Source/provider timestamps alone cannot backdate visibility. Safety barrier discovery considers all committed visible correction events at snapshot acquisition, even if the caller passes an older business cutoff; it cannot be evaded by requesting a fresh snapshot with a pre-correction cutoff. The historic result is available only by replaying its already persisted immutable snapshot.

Clearing requires a newly appended, fully qualified source-object assessment which records the correction event/version it resolved and independently proves authority at receipt from the new evidence. A later event affecting that selected authority blocks again. A pending reassessment, reviewer acknowledgment, new directory flag, or bare replacement capture cannot clear the barrier. `reviewReceivedStructuralSource`/`reviewReceivedClosureSource` own explicit source reassessment under their current permissions and complete business checks; committed-Z04 evidence must retain its existing genuine handoff requirements. If reassessment cannot be established, leave blocked, not automatically accepted.

Keep old snapshot bytes/hashes and old source assessments untouched. Existing nonheld UTILTS reservations/replays retain their recorded outcome and consumption binding; this correction never retroactively rewrites ACKs or quantities. A new UTILTS invocation receives the new barrier and holds the affected transaction; an eligible identical-byte held retry may requalify only after an explicit qualified successor clears it. Previously completed business-side correction/reconciliation is a separate authorized operation, not an implicit side effect of authority publication.

Add these decisive native+runtime cases beyond the generic revocation test:

- Accept source A with V1; snapshot S1 selects A. Publish genuine retrospective V2 covering A's receipt. Snapshot S2 must hold A and must not fall back to an earlier assessment/source; replay S1 is byte/hash identical. Repeat for a closure and verify supply is not revived by dropping the closure.
- Publish expiry only after A's receipt: S2 still selects A. Publish an event with unknown affected extent: S2 holds A conservatively. An unrelated principal/source remains unaffected when completeness is proven.
- Two real transactions interleave event publication and snapshot/assessment capture on both sides of commit. No snapshot sees published V2 without its barrier; late V1-dependent assessment is held in later snapshots. Requesting a new snapshot with an old cutoff does not bypass the visible barrier.
- Omit/forge a dependency; exceed a discovery bound; place dependent sources in two permitted tenant scopes and an unrelated tenant. Native append rejects missing linkage, new readset cannot claim completeness on truncation, each permitted scope blocks its own dependent source and returns no other tenant's records.
- After V2, qualified successor assessment resolves the event and only a new snapshot becomes usable. Cached JSON approval cannot clear it. Nonheld reservation remains immutable; identical-byte held replay follows the new snapshot and preserves consumption-binding checks.
