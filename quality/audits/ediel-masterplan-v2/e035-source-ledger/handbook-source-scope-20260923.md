## Design conclusions

**The changed-start source gap is narrowed substantially.** Replace the proposed generic “date-changing Z04/BGM5 owner” direction with a bounded *Z04 cancellation plus new confirmation* lifecycle design for the documented LK process. P42 BGM5 syntax remains valid generally; it does not override Handbok's explicit lifecycle sequence here. Do not treat a same-LI new Z04 as a direct predecessor replacement without the witnessed Z04C edge and original Z03 correlation. The bare same-case BGM5 date move remains held.

The required new owner should retain the old committed supply/root, authenticate the exact Z04C target, commit cancellation of that start, then bind the new Z04LK to original outbound Z03 and its own actual successful lifecycle outcome. Preserve knowledge cutoffs and independent coverage of the new period; never mutate the old immutable baseline or infer coverage through a cancelled start. For changed Z03 inputs the upstream cancellation/new request is a different chain; do not collapse it into the old Z03 case. Already-effective corrections and internal sender-system corrections need their actual process/contact evidence rather than mechanically applying a generic compensation rule.

**Bounded Z05 closure/cancellation designs remain directionally correct, but cannot be called universally sufficient.** The named immutable Z05C cancellation edge and separate witness match the recovered process. The following amendments are material before extending acceptance:

- New corrected-date Z05 is a *new case with new LI*. Do not require it to share cancelled Z05's LI or fold it into BGM5 same-case replacement. Its new closure must attach to the still-valid supply lineage after the qualified cancellation. A pending new closure/ambiguous chain must not yield an unbounded reopened interval by dropping either event.
- Retire the cancelled closure case as an authority for reading correlation. The structural selector's meter/register state alone does not certify this separate reference requirement. Add exact cancelled-LI versus new-LI consumer tests at the actual UTILTS correlation owner; no automatic new national error code may be invented from local uncertainty.
- Sub-day values remain necessary *for SQL forgery/binding tests* because DTM203 encodes minutes. For the next bounded closure business positives use00:00. The direct source establishes the L supplier-exchange handover; it does **not**, by itself, establish a universal midnight restriction on LK move-out/decommissioning or every other ending. Do not introduce a nationwide LK rejection from this paragraph. Keep lexical12:34/12:35 mutation probes separate from business positives; sub-day LK acceptance needs its own actual process qualification. This narrows the approved test scope rather than proving the exact-minute parser wrong.
- Do not let “same supply now active/source=C” alone authorize undoing an otherwise valid rescission. Carry enough process context to distinguish correcting an erroneous closure from restarting service after rescission/payment; the latter requires a new contract/switch. Until that owner is qualified, the ambiguous context should remain local review. This is not a blanket assertion that every Z05 linked to Z08H is uncancellable.
- Date-equality between C and its named old closure remains a conservative supported subset, not a newly proved universal syntax requirement. The *new* closure carries the new stop date. Keep explicit contact provenance for retroactive changes where required; do not send messages automatically from this discovery.

## Concrete C-owner gap after this recovery

The closure L/LK marker itself is not contradicted by the new source. The proposed generic cancellation marker has a real **design omission**: requiring any actual legacy active/source=C write still cannot distinguish a permissible erroneous-end correction from an impermissible restart-after-valid-rescission. That operational write is not a cause/status owner. Amend C acceptance before implementation; do not treat this report as a reason to remove any current source hold.

Reusable *signals*, not automatic approvals: original witnessedZ05 and its LI; authentic tenant/environment-scoped outboundZ08H original whose legal parties/object/LI/exact end bind that target; contract/supply lineage; actual persisted contract termination events. The inspected source does not supply an express general ruling for every expired/invalid rescission. Do not label those automatically permissible or prohibited. If the target was itself erroneous, the existing erroneous-end process is a possible route, but it needs evidence that this particular end was erroneous and the specific valid correction workflow; later payment alone is not that evidence.
