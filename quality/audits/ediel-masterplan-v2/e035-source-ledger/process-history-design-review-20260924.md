# E035 Task3 process-history design gate

Read-only design review, 2026-09-24. Reviewed the preflight against approved C-PB-1/3 and actual shared transport, direct orchestrator, outbox sender, SMTP outcome classifier and email helper. No live calls/sends, runtime/schema/memory edits, tests, commits or delegation. Current sealed-source Task2 is unaffected.

**SPEC: NEEDS BOUNDED AMENDMENTS. QUALITY: SOUND DIRECTION; NOT YET A CLOSED IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT.** Mandatory pre-provider reservation at the shared boundary is the correct place to cover direct and worker sends. Three owner sets, explicit inception/retention gaps, no historical dispatch invention and no positive-C authority are also correct. The following details need closure before implementing Task3.

## PH-1 — Close reservation ownership and terminal retry rules

The proposal guarantees exclusion “while unresolved” but must also prohibit resend after accepted evidence when mutable status remains queued because projection failed. State an immutable, per-source reservation/state machine: preparation, exclusive provider-entry transition, observed result, independently witnessed result and separately authorized reconciliation. Accepted or partially accepted submission remains permanently no-resend for that original; unresolved provider entry is no-resend even if outbox claims expire or status is reset. Repair status/outbox projections from existing evidence without invoking SMTP. A new outbox attempt ID or MIME/route override must not evade the per-original guard.

The DB provider-entry transition must be conditional on current reservation ownership and return a one-time proceed decision. Two callers holding the same attempt ID must not both enter the provider; a stale worker must fail its fence after ownership changes. Specify safe release of preparation that provably never entered the provider, and explicit all-recipient rejection retry policy; neither may make an old paused invocation eligible later. Bind worker attempt ID to actual claimed outbox row/message/company rather than trusting a parameter. Direct actor comes from authenticated server context; service wrapper/SQL recheck tenant and authorization. Add races for duplicate same-attempt calls, stale prepared worker resuming after release, accepted evidence with queued projection, and direct/worker route changes.

Evidence: shared function has five sendEdielEmail branches; worker currently sets providerAccepted only after transport returns (sendOutboxItem.ts254–258), and direct outbox supersession occurs after transport (orchestrator.ts424–445). Existing mutable status checks cannot close these windows.

## PH-2 — Separate partial acceptance, rejection and physical-byte identity

Explicitly classify nonempty accepted plus nonempty rejected recipient sets as **partial acceptance/no automatic full resend**, not terminal non-delivery. Current transport treats rejected.length>0 as ordinary failure before its post-acceptance try/catch (index.part-2.ts758–777); the new wrapper must record any returned provider acceptance before this branch and preserve duplicate suppression even when its failed-event append also fails. A negative SMTP response, pre-connect failure, ambiguous DATA/socket loss and successful acceptance are different observations. Test partial acceptance and malformed/empty provider results as well as existing timeout/rejection cases. Unrecognized outcomes after provider-entry stay uncertain; a generic exception is not proof of no submission.

Record exact **encoded bytes**, not only normalized JavaScript text: transport uses latin1 Buffer conversion for EDIFACT and UTF-8 for other payloads. DB hashing normalized text as UTF-8 would not match transmitted non-ASCII EDIFACT bytes. Save encoding, bounded byte count and separate canonical-original and actual EDIFACT-byte hashes; validate route/envelope recipient binding. Packaging/archiving errors before provider invocation must remain distinct from provider response, while conservative provider-entry marking may intentionally leave an uncertainty hold. This ledger records provider observation time, never exact unobserved provider acceptance time, delivery or legal rescission validity.

## PH-3 — Define scoped candidate universe before completeness limits

“Count before filtering” needs precision: count all **potentially relevant candidates inside authenticated tenant/environment and conservative process scope before semantic acceptance filtering**, not every same-company historical record. A large proven-unrelated point must not overflow another point's readset or impose a company-wide hold. Return scoped failure/overflow with the known scope and unknown fields as wildcards. Retention/producer coverage gaps attach to the owner/process/object interval they actually affect; only genuinely unbounded same-context coverage uncertainty may broaden that hold.

Define scope evidence separately from semantic eligibility. Validated immutable object/agency/parties/process linkage may prove unrelatedness; a conflicting mutable customer link, malformed party/date, invalid reference or failed canonical assessment cannot safely exclude a potentially relevant same-point record. Such a record can block but never authorize dispatch/legal cause/coverage. A wholly unrelated, positively identified record stays irrelevant even if its own assessment is rejected or its history unavailable. Distinguish cross-tenant authorization failure (no foreign disclosure) from unresolved linkage within the authorized tenant.

Require a scope matrix: same LI/different proved point; same point/missing customer or party; conflicting point/customer links; malformed original; semantically rejected same-point context; unrelated point with overflow/missing retention; same-context missing inception; global owner outage with no reliable finer scope. Assert no false positive authority and no unjustified global hold in each. Preserve C-PB-3 raw/rejected/unwitnessed concern visibility and earliest-plausible-boundary/unknown-boundary hold independently of accepted dispatch classification. A Z08H-only eligibility gate must not silently discard an ambiguous candidate whose subtype could not be established.

## PH-4 — Finish finite producer and coverage map before third-set completeness

The preflight candidly says supply/case/contract commands are not fully enumerated. That is the remaining design gap, not an implementation detail to discover after claiming complete:true. Supply a finite mandatory map for each of the three sets: original/transition producer, every direct/RPC/batch/legacy mutation route, capture transaction boundary, actor/tenant scope, byte/row limits, epoch, deletion/archive behavior and failure visibility. For customer/contract/process transitions, atomic capture inside the actual DB command/trigger is preferable where supported; a best-effort post-command hook that can disappear on crash cannot support completeness. A relevant producer must either commit an immutable observation atomically, leave a durable scoped gap or prevent the business transition; define which applies.

The saved readset must materialize all three owner sets and their counts/coverage/witnesses under one actual database visibility boundary; separate client queries sharing only a timestamp do not satisfy this. Unknown pre-epoch history, archive loss, an unavailable required producer or failed initial capture remains complete:false until genuine independently retained history is reconciled. Post-epoch/no-error is not automatically coverage from process inception. Explicitly prohibit using a mutable current event/status row or source statement claiming “sent” to backfill missing dispatch or historical absence.

Bounded phases may implement named producers while the third set remains incomplete. That is acceptable engineering, but must be labelled partial and cannot unlock positive process-basis decisions. Preserve unrelated-scope behavior from PH-3 when a particular producer lacks history.

## Conclusion and limits

No new retention duration or legal policy is requested. The existing categories cannot establish actual retained coverage, and this review does not invent historical sends or rescission semantics. The preflight's after-acceptance failure and no-retry principles are retained; amendments make their enforcement and completeness claims concrete. After PH-1..4 are incorporated, rereview can approve a bounded implementation contract. None requires pausing the independently proceeding sealed-Z05 Task2.

### Binding cross-task activation contract — C-PB-1/3

The approval above is for **conditional correction-process history**, not a universal new prerequisite for every ordinary structural source or historical supply. Carry this explicit rule into Task3/4 briefs:

1. Activate the new history requirement for a process/supply/object with an actual potentially relevant correction/cancellation concern visible at the selected knowledge cutoff, or for a future request to grant correction/basis/C authority. This includes raw, rejected, unwitnessed and ambiguously linked potentially matching concerns; it does not require accepted semantics. A durable failed-capture/producer-gap signal known to reach that correction context also activates scoped incompleteness.
2. Once activated, all three required sets need actual inception-to-cutoff coverage. Missing epoch/history, overflow or producer failure cannot prove absence of conflicting Z08H and holds the affected correction scope. No positive C is available in this slice regardless of history success.
3. An unrelated ordinary source/supply must keep its existing qualified behavior when no potentially matching correction context reaches it. The mere fact that it began before the new history producer epoch is **not** an activation signal and must not impose a new universal hold. A proven unrelated point's gap is likewise irrelevant.
4. If an actually observed concern or known failed producer has uncertain object/customer/date linkage, use its known tenant/environment/party/process dimensions and wildcard missing dimensions; broaden only to potentially matching scopes. Do not manufacture an observed correction concern from the existence of a new epoch alone. Unknown whole-tenant scope is justified only by an actual concern/gap whose reach cannot be narrowed, not by generic absence of historical instrumentation.
5. Preserve saved earlier cutoffs and finalized results. Later concern capture must not backdate a hold into an earlier snapshot. Before positive C, the scoped response is unavailable/no invented national mismatch or positive coverage, with earliest plausible boundary or unknown-boundary hold as C-PB-3 requires.

The amended preflight does not state this runtime activation boundary as explicitly as its scope matrix. Treat these five points as a **required clarification of the approved bounded implementation contract**, not authorization for a company-wide epoch gate. Required paired oracle: ordinary previously qualified supply with no matching concern remains unaffected despite pre-epoch inception; the identical scope with a newly visible matching raw/unwitnessed correction concern holds for missing required history. Add proven-unrelated and wildcard-scope variants. Task4 integration is not approved if it omits this distinction.
