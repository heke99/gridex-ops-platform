## Capture/trigger order and deletion checks

- Inspect native `pg_trigger`, `pg_constraint` and column defaults/generated expressions after full replay before fixtures. Same-kind triggers run by name; capture final INSERT/UPDATE after BEFORE normalizers. Customer contracts have `aa_` identity, `ab_` pricing, `ac_` area; quote/publication/state/availability/tenant-snapshot gates; final `zz_` canonical-binding gate, and AFTER partner/signed-operation writers. Capture must include their actual committed side effects, not bypass them.
- DELETE capture must use OLD and previously immutable captured link facts. A parent BEFORE-delete capture can retain graph before referential actions; child cascade capture cannot assume parent remains queryable. Any capture inserted before a later business rejection must roll back with the transaction. SET NULL actions need OLD+NEW capture too, especially tenant/customer nullification on contract events/tasks and job/site/point nullification on operation events.
- Native minimum: direct CRUD for all twelve; case→event cascade; customer→case/event/job cascade on a graph without restrictive contracts/supplies/sites; site→job cascade in an isolated valid graph; point→job/event SET NULL; job→event SET NULL; restricted switch-with-event delete; draft contract deletion after dependencies removed; signed-contract delete rejection; archive partial failure with prior committed tombstones. Do not describe a whole customer graph deletion as guaranteed: concurrent restrictive FKs matter.
- Ledger/tombstone rows must remain readable by authorized tenant+saved object scope after business parents disappear; avoid a cascading ledger FK to those parents. Native denial checks for anon/authenticated direct private access, wrong-company links and disabled-trigger/TRUNCATE paths are qualification obligations, not accomplished here.
