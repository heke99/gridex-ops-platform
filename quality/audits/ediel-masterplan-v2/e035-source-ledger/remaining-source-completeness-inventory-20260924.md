Before applying these owners to an earlier date, first resolve the then-effective PRODAT/technical/handbook edition and any applicable transition/predecessor provisions; this local inventory has no full original pre-2026-04 PRODAT, no complete earlier technical/handbook edition chain, and no prior U before 2025-06.
