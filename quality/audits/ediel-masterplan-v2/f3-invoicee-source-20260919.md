## Decision for root review

A coherent protocol implementation is **six IV parents plus their 36 children**,
using one per-object comparison/party policy and additive body-bound evidence.
A parent-only implementation leaves child252 incorrectly coupled to the parent;
a252-only implementation leaves parent selection and all other children without
independent authority. The smallest complete unit therefore includes all42,
subject to root approving the explicit source supplement below. Live customer
selection and bilateral authority remain separate, unqualified work.

**Do not treat eligible equal-address IV as forbidden.** Fresh original p109 explicitly
requires both addresses when the invoice address has changed to match the end
user's address. Current frozen PG-IV and the root-boolean engine omit this event.
The source contract must compose independently selected current address values
with an independently sourced changed-to-same event for grid-owner-to-supplier
Z06E/E34. This OR decides mandatory inclusion, not exhaustive permission: eligible
equal/no-event IV is not forbidden. Reuse explicit invoicee object/null choice,
then activate children from actual eligible parent use. An event inferred from
equal output is not evidence. If root defers
this event contract, the independently reviewable alternative is five codes
(Z03/Z04/Z05/Z08/Z09: five parents +30children); leave all seven Z06 occurrences
unaccepted rather than silently applying the wrong equality branch.

C058 and country subdivision3229 are unused; extra components/elements cannot
be flattened into allowed values. Field250 is not LIN209, and invoicee identity
is not assumed to equal UD227. Different identity/name alone is not address
difference. A different invoice recipient can have the same address; a shared
identity can have different addresses.

The address comparison concerns selected **end-user versus invoicee** postal
addresses, not installation versus billing, not old versus new address (except
the event), and not the presence of NAD+IV. P117–118 describe street/box/c-o and
separate postcode/city; p79/82 give each party's country. Comparing the postal
tuple (three address positions, postcode, city, country) is the source-grounded
implementation interpretation. The document does **not** supply a general
address-equivalence/canonicalization algorithm. Do not claim byte inequality of
arbitrarily formatted source strings proves different real addresses. Require
independently selected, comparable component representations; mixed combined/
split lines, country-code aliases or unresolved source representation remain U
until the producer qualifies them. The selection must retain a producer-defined
common representation convention/source reference and the p117 separation mode;
a bare `comparable:true` assertion is insufficient. Producer checks must establish
that both selected tuples use that convention. An explicit operator/TGT assertion
of the convention is test provenance, not a new address standard or live proof.
No case folding, punctuation stripping, line sorting, blank compaction, fuzzy
matching or site-address substitution. Equality requires **all compared
components known and equal** under the qualified shared representation; equality
of only the known subset is insufficient. A known comparable differing component
establishes different even if another component is unknown; insufficient
comparison remains U.

Known-empty is a positive source fact and is distinct from unavailable and
unknown. In particular,252 unavailable means the sender cannot supply that
address value; it does not prove the real street is empty. A raw blank workbook
cell establishes neither known-empty nor availability. The representation must
preserve these states per component. Exclude the p118 wire dot from semantic
comparison and availability evidence; retain the raw selected street slot.

### Four separate decisions: eligibility, mandatory inclusion, choice, children

`R` required; `X` explicitly excluded/inapplicable; `U` insufficient outbound
knowledge. “Not required” is not a literal O edit to the frozen matrix and does
not imply a prohibition. Frozen numeric cells250/251/253/317/318 use
parent-active => R, inactive => X;252 uses parent-active AND address-applicable
=> R, false => X. Those child rows do not establish when the parent is forbidden.

1. **Eligibility:** the six-code matrix and Z09 E-only restriction control
   whether the group may be used. Exact own first-register reason determines
   Z09 eligibility; missing/ambiguous/invalid reason stays unresolved/invalid.
   Eligibility is not authorization to originate a live business message.
2. **Source-requiredness:** for an eligible object, `D` is independently known
   current address difference; `E` is the independent changed-to-same event in
   the exact grid-owner-to-supplier Z06E process. `mandatory = D OR E` uses
   three-valued logic. Outside that process E is not applicable/false; do not
   require invented history for other codes. Contradictory facts are invalid.
3. **Explicit pre-wire choice:** reuse invoicee object/null and snapshot clear
   behavior as include/omit choice, adding analogous object inputs where absent.
   No redundant root selection boolean. This choice is neither source authority
   nor proof of D/E. An eligible, source-qualified IV may be explicitly included
   when not mandatory. Default omission is a producer choice, not a prohibition.
4. **Rendered-wire child activation:** actual eligible IV use activates its
   children, independently of why inclusion was mandatory. Wire presence is
   descriptive evidence of use, never evidence of D/E. Pre-wire and rendered
   phases must be explicit in diagnostics, as with accepted optional IT.

| Eligibility and independent facts | Source-requiredness | Choice and child consequences |
|---|---|---|
|Code outside six, or Z09 own valid non-E reason|Excluded X|Do not emit IV; a present parent is forbidden and children cannot legalize it.|
|Z09 reason missing/ambiguous/invalid|U / reason invalid|No root fallback and no proven inactive parent.|
|Eligible, current addresses differ (D=true)|R|IV omission is an error; if emitted five children required and252 follows its own state. Event history is not needed to prove D=true.|
|Exact Z06E process, independent event true (E=true)|R|Both current IV/UD addresses must agree; IV omission is an error; emitted children follow their own rules.|
|Eligible, D=false and E=false/not applicable|Not required|Explicit qualified omission leaves children inactive/X; explicit qualified inclusion activates all five unconditional children and independent252. No equal-address prohibition.|
|Eligible, insufficient D/E to decide OR|U|Omission, rootfalse or emitted IV cannot prove source-requiredness. If IV is present, validate its children while retaining source U.|

For every eligible IV actually sent,250/251/253/317/318 are required even when
D/E are false or source-requiredness is unresolved.252 is independently R when
its selected address is applicable/available; explicitly unavailable means omit
populated C059; unknown remains unresolved for own outbound qualification.
Absent IV with known mandatory=true is missing. Absent IV with qualified
mandatory=false is a permitted omission and children are inapplicable. Unknown
mandatory cannot be represented as a proven inactive parent.

Source-qualification U is not itself proof of a wire protocol violation. Own
outbound controls may block unknown/missing provenance and protect that block
from intentional-invalid test overrides. Inbound must not be rejected solely
because local comparison/history is unavailable; do not infer an event from an
incoming equal pair. Present-parent child and structural/scope checks remain
wire-evaluable.

P23 does not restrict Z06 IV to E: Z06F/G may need independent UD comparison
values despite no UD on wire. P109's exception is specifically Z06E grid-to-
supplier; it cannot enable a supplier-only Z06 facade. P112 expressly describes
Z09E as death notification; pre-existing broader bilateral-purpose wording in
`businessSemantics.ts` is not permission for this IV task. Preserve the bounded
E-only group rule without certifying or widening the Z09E business process.
The p109 supplement and this separation are for root review; originals remain
unchanged.

   this binding rather than accept a bare comparability boolean. IV252
