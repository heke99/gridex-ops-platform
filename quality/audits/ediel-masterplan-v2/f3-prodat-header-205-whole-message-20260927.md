## Source, owner and effects

P26.A r3, effective 2026-04-01, §2.2 p16 requires field 205 (`DTM+137/C507/2380`, format 203) for all 13 PRODAT message codes. P-APERAK 16.B pp90,95,98–103 distinguishes BGM/1225=27 for exceptional rejection of the entire message from 34 for a processed message; a header error has at least one ERC per discovered error. ERC41 denotes missing required data, ERC42 invalid content, with real field number in FTX. Masterplan CV-P-HEADER, ACK-02/ACK-10 and §§18–20 preserve syntax versus national application decisions. The original BGM/1004 remains RFF+ACW; the reply's UNH is its own identity. The source PDF extraction is `permission-ack-source-20260920/original-relevant-pages.txt`.

`prodat26AFieldMatrix` and `validateCanonicalPolicyFields` own the date rule. `prodatDateState` checks the first header, value, format and submitted failure components. `projectProdatDiagnostics` produces typed ERC41/42 and its physical occurrence. `prodatHeader205Rejection` checks a complete P wire and the exact source-rule, original UNH, error class and invalid component evidence. Both `renderAperakEdiel` and the actual `processInboundEdielMessage` consume that qualification. A detached fragment is not silently promoted to an entire-message national error. A positive or foreign/fabricated 205 response is held. LIN/314 from #414 remains an independent qualified whole-message cause; a processed object-local 213 remains 34.

The inbound source belongs to the resolved tenant. The existing canonical ACK gateway resolves the legal sender and Ediel ACK route/profile; this PR grants no actor mandate or new route. `ediel_messages` owns stored source and ACK bytes/related identity, and `ediel_outbox` owns a tenant/route-bound immutable ACK intent and retry lock. For header rejection, the consumer emits positive technical CONTRL and one negative P-APERAK, then returns before facility recognition, case creation, switch, supply or business state. If no qualified route exists, the existing ACK guard holds the send. No transport worker, staging, TGT/AGT, counterparty or live market send is run.
